== 5. What went wrong in the test, said plainly ==
This phone's own settings hold picked sounds for two classes (localStorage wmSounds.ev: notification = Alarm5.wav,
information = Alarm2.wav). A class with a pick of its own keeps it - that is the rule - so two generic test-lane notices
(class "notification") shown by the page at 00:56:46 and 00:58:16 played wm61/Alarm5.wav, at notification volume 1 of 15.
One was my own page-in-front test notice (sent without a cls), the other another agent's lane notice. I then set the
notification volume to 0 for the rest, sent my last test notice as cls "reminder", and told the other agents.
The picker previews in the walk therefore ran at volume 0 (the app's player started; nothing was audible).
