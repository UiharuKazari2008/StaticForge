Annual summaries

Annual summaries are compiled from the weekly sample periods ending in each calendar year (sample change on Tuesdays). A summary year therefore contains 52 or 53 periods and covers 364 or 371 days. Concentrations are precipitation-weighted means of valid samples; deposition is the weighted mean concentration multiplied by the total gauge precipitation for the same periods. Values for 2026 will be published after the last period of the year has been released.

Years in grey italics do not meet the completeness criteria (see Quality assurance) and should not be used for trend analysis. They are shown so that the record is complete.
