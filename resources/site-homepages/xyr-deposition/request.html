Data requests

Annual summaries are available without registration on the Data page. Use this form to request weekly sample records, data before 2017, or the Dreamscape Valley comparison data. Data are provided free of charge for research, teaching and non-commercial use. Requests are normally answered within ten working days by the station data steward, Yukimi Kazari. Requests from outside Academy City go through the routine data export review before data are sent, which usually adds about a week.
