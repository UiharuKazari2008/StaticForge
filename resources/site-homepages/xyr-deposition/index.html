Station overview

The Xyridian Ridge station (XYR) is a long-term wet deposition monitoring site operated by the Environmental Monitoring Section of Academy City Research. Precipitation is collected weekly with a wet-only collector and analysed for pH, conductivity and the major ions. The station keeps a long-term record of the acidity and chemistry of precipitation reaching the catchment of the District 21 water-supply reservoirs, the reservoirs behind the dams that supply Academy City's water.

Station codeXYR
LocationCrest of Xyridian Ridge, School District 21, Academy City, on the watershed above the upper reservoirs. About 4 km north-west of the Nishikubo forestry depot. Access by the ridge track from the reservoir service road (locked gate; site visits by arrangement only).
Elevation812 m above sea level
SiteMown grass clearing in mixed broadleaf forest. The collector inlet is 1.0 m above ground; the nearest trees are more than twice their height from the collector.
SamplingWet-only precipitation, weekly sample change on Tuesday mornings. Co-located weighing precipitation gauge.
MeasurementspH, conductivity, SO42-, NO3-, Cl-, NH4+, Na+, K+, Ca2+, Mg2+; precipitation depth
RecordApril 2009 to present. Current collector in service since December 2016; summaries on this site start in 2017.
StatusOperating. Most recent released sample period: XYR-2026-031 (28 July 2026 to 4 August 2026).

Related site

From April 2014 to March 2017 a second collector of the same type was operated at the Dreamscape Valley pumping station (station DSV, 214 m), in a lower valley in the east of District 21, as a lowland comparison site. Those data were not part of the routine programme and are not shown here; they can be requested through the data request form.
