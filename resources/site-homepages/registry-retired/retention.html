Xyridian Sample Registry - retired service

Retention and disposal statement

Document RDS-0352, issued 30 June 2023. This statement records what happened to each group of XSR records and the authority under which records were kept or destroyed. It is issued under the Academy City Research Records Management Policy (RM-02).

Download the statement (PDF, 43 KB)

Retention classes applied

ClassDescriptionRule
7.1Closed research sample recordsRetain permanently in the institutional archive (Archive Level B7, School District 22)
7.2Active sample recordsResponsibility of the depositing group while work continues
7.3Pilot and feasibility study recordsRetain 5 years after project close, then destroy
9.4System test and training dataNo retention value; delete when the system is withdrawn

Method of disposal

Records in classes 7.3 and 9.4 were deleted from the XSR database and its backup sets, and the storage media were sanitised by the Research Computing service. A certificate of destruction for class 7.3 is held on the Records Office disposal file.

Review

This statement will be reviewed on 31 March 2027, when the retirement notice is withdrawn.
