Xyridian Sample Registry - retired service

Requesting copies of archived records

Records transferred from the Xyridian Sample Registry are held in the Academy City Research institutional archive, Archive Level B7, School District 22. They are not available online and the archive has no public reading room. You can ask for copies in writing, using the online form or the printed form.

What to include

  • your full name and a postal address for our reply;
  • the full record reference for each record (XSR-YYYY-NNNNN), or, if you do not have it, the depositing group and the approximate year;
  • your former XSR username, if you had an account;
  • whether you want electronic or paper copies.

Proof of identity

Archived records can only be released to the group that deposited them, or to someone acting with that group's written authority. We will check your name against the list of closed XSR accounts. If you did not have an account, enclose a copy of one document showing your name and address, or a letter on headed paper from your organisation confirming your role. Please do not send original documents.

How long it takes

We acknowledge requests within 5 working days and reply in full within 28 working days of receiving everything we need. The 28 days start again if we have to ask you for more information.

Letters posted from outside Academy City are routed through the goods and mail transfer point in School District 11, where incoming and outgoing mail is inspected. Please allow about 5 extra working days for your request to reach us, and the same again for our reply to reach you.

Fees

There is no charge for electronic copies. Paper copies of up to 100 pages are also free. For larger paper requests we will write to you with the cost before any copying is done.
