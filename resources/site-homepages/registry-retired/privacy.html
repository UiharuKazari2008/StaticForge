Privacy notice: requests for archived XSR records

Document RDS-0353, last reviewed 2 September 2026. This notice explains how Academy City Research uses the information you give when you ask for copies of archived Xyridian Sample Registry records.

Who is responsible

Academy City Research is responsible for this information. Requests are handled by the Records Office within Research Data Services.

What we collect

  • your name, postal address and organisation;
  • your former XSR username, if you give it;
  • the records you ask for and any details you provide;
  • copies of identity documents or letters of authority you send us.

How we use it

Only to confirm that you are entitled to the records, to find them, to send you copies and to keep a record of what was released. We do not use it for any other purpose and we do not send marketing.

Who sees it

Records Office staff and, where a request concerns another group's records, the lead of that group, so that they can confirm or refuse authority. We do not pass your information to anyone else unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Request files are kept for 2 years after the request is closed (schedule class 2.1) and then destroyed. Copies of identity documents are destroyed once your identity has been confirmed.

Your choices

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it. Write to the Records Office at the address on the Requesting copies page. Concerns about how your information has been handled can be raised in writing with the Academy City Research Information Governance Office at the same address.

This website

This website does not use cookies. Apart from the request form, it does not collect any information about visitors.
