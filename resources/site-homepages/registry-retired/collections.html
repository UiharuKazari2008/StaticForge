Xyridian Sample Registry - retired service

What happened to XSR collections

At the date of retirement XSR held 50,227 records in five groups. Each group was assessed against the Academy City Research Records Retention Schedule (version 4.1) before the service closed.

Transferred

45,319 records (90.2%) were transferred to the Academy City Research institutional archive, Archive Level B7, School District 22, on 31 March 2023. They are kept permanently and can be requested in writing. See Requesting copies.

Returned

Records still open at retirement were exported to the depositing groups, which remain responsible for them. Please contact the group directly; the Records Office does not hold copies.

Not migrated

The Dreamscape pilot collection held records from a feasibility study that closed in March 2018. Under schedule class 7.3, pilot and feasibility records are kept for 5 years after the project closes. That period ended in March 2023, no extension was requested, and the records were destroyed on 14 June 2023. Test and training entries had no retention value and were deleted when the system was withdrawn.

No copies of records in these two groups remain. Requests for them cannot be fulfilled. The full statement is on the retention and disposal page.

Samples

XSR held records about samples, not the samples themselves. Questions about physical material should go to the group that held it.
