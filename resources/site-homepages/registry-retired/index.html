The Xyridian Sample Registry has been retired

The Xyridian Sample Registry (XSR) was decommissioned on 31 March 2023 as part of the Research Data Services records consolidation programme. It is no longer possible to search or view sample records through this website.

Closed records held in XSR were transferred to the Academy City Research institutional archive. Requests for copies must be made in writing and should include the full record reference, in the form XSR-2014-03315. Please allow 28 working days for a response. Requests by telephone or email cannot be accepted.

If you were a registered XSR user, your account has been closed and no further action is needed.

General enquiries about this notice: +1 (997) 434-2600 (Monday to Thursday, 09:30 to 16:00).
