Test kit accuracy

Each tester's differential pressure test kit must be verified for accuracy at least once every 12 months by a District-approved facility, and again after it is repaired, dropped or frozen. The verification date is recorded on every test report. Approved facilities:

  • Xyridian Instrument Calibration, 220 Gauge Works Lane, Pellham Cross
  • Brannock Gauge & Meter Service, 61 Foundry Row, Westmere

Becoming a certified tester

New testers must pass the District written and practical examinations, held twice a year at the District Office. Ask for Form BF-3 at the counter or by the contact form. Current testers renew each year with Form BF-5 (PDF, 53 KB).
