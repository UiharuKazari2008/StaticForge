Backflow Program Forms

Forms are printable PDF files. Print them single-sided on letter paper and complete them in ink. Completed forms may be mailed to P.O. Box 2318, Harrow Vale, left in the drop box at the District Office, or handed in at the counter. The District does not accept test reports by e-mail.

FormWho uses itWhen
Annual Backflow Prevention Assembly Test Report, Form BF-1 (PDF, 85 KB)Certified testerEvery test: annual, new installation, after repair. One assembly per form; detector assemblies need a second form for the bypass assembly.
Backflow Assembly Installation, Replacement or Removal Notification, Form BF-2 (PDF, 58 KB)Owner and installerAt least 5 business days before an assembly is installed, replaced, relocated or removed.
Tester Certification Renewal Checklist and Application 2026-2027, Form BF-5 (PDF, 53 KB)Certified testerAt least 30 days before your certificate expires.

Forms available from the counter

These forms are not posted on the website. Ask at the counter, call +1 (997) 434-2600, ext. 4418, or request one with the contact form and it will be mailed to you.

  • Form BF-3, New Tester Certification Application (includes examination dates)
  • Form BF-4, Request for Extension of Test Due Date
  • Approved Backflow Assembly List (makes, models and sizes that may be installed in the District)

Common reasons a test report is returned

  • Test kit accuracy verification more than 12 months before the test date.
  • Tester certificate expired on the test date.
  • Serial number does not match the assembly on record. If the assembly was replaced, a Form BF-2 is also needed.
  • Relief valve opening point not recorded for an RP assembly.
  • Report not signed.
