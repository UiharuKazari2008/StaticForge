Board of Directors - Agendas and Minutes

The Board of Directors meets on the second Wednesday of each month at 6:00 p.m. in the Board Room, District Office, 4100 Settling Pond Road. Meetings are open to the public. Agendas are posted on the bulletin board outside the District Office and on this page at least 72 hours before a regular meeting.

Agendas and minutes marked "On file" are kept at the District Office and may be inspected during office hours. Copies are $0.15 per page. Minutes are posted here after the Board approves them, usually at the following meeting.

To address the Board on an item not on the agenda, speak during Public Comment (3 minutes per speaker). Written comments may be sent with the contact form; choose "Board of Directors" and they will be given to the Board Clerk.
