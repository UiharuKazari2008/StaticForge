How to Pay Your Water and Sewer Bill

Bills are mailed every two months. Payment is due 21 days after the billing date printed on the bill. Please include the bill stub or write your account number on your check.

The District does not offer online, card-by-internet or telephone payment at this time.

By mail

Mail the bill stub with a check or money order payable to Harrow Vale Water Reclamation District No. 7 to:

Harrow Vale Water Reclamation District No. 7
Customer Accounts
P.O. Box 2318
Harrow Vale

Allow at least 5 days for mail. Payments are posted on the day they are received. Do not send cash by mail.

Drop box

A payment drop box is at the front entrance of the District Office, 4100 Settling Pond Road. It is emptied at 8:00 a.m. Monday to Thursday. Payments left on a Friday, Saturday or Sunday are posted the following Monday, and payments left on a District holiday are posted on the next business day. Checks and money orders only.

At the counter

District Office, 4100 Settling Pond Road, Harrow Vale. Monday to Thursday, 7:00 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. Closed Fridays and District holidays. Cash, checks and money orders are accepted. The counter cannot accept debit or credit cards.

Automatic bank draft

The amount due can be drafted from a checking or savings account on the due date. Pick up an enrollment form at the counter, or ask for one with the contact form and it will be mailed to you. Return it with a voided check. Enrollment takes effect on the next bill after the form is processed, so please pay the current bill in the usual way.

Charges

ItemCharge
Late charge, balance unpaid after the due date$12.00
Returned check or rejected bank draft$25.00
Service restoration after suspension, business hours$65.00

If you cannot pay the full amount by the due date, ask at the counter or call +1 (997) 434-2600 about a payment arrangement before the due date. Payments cannot be taken over the telephone.
