Construction Notices

Notices of District construction and maintenance work that may affect water service, traffic or access. Customers directly affected also receive a door hanger at least 48 hours before any planned water shutoff.

Dreamscape Estates Water Main Replacement, Phase 1

Notice CN-26-16 | Posted October 19, 2026 | Contract No. 2026-04

Streets: Lark Hollow Drive, Tamsin Court, Vellum Way and Orchard Bend Lane.

Work: Replacement of about 4,850 feet of 6-inch cast iron water main installed in 1962 with new 8-inch PVC main, 14 new fire hydrants, and new service lines from the main to each meter (96 services). The Board awarded the contract to Tamarack Pipeline Constructors on October 14, 2026.

Schedule: Work is expected to start the week of Monday, November 16, 2026, and to be substantially complete in March 2027, weather permitting. Work hours are 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday. The contractor works Fridays; District staff do not.

What to expect:

  • Most homes will be served from a temporary above-ground water line along the curb while the new main is built. Please do not drive over or move the temporary line.
  • Each home will have two or three short shutoffs, usually under 4 hours, when it is moved to the temporary line and later to the new main.
  • The new main is flushed, disinfected and sampled, and must pass bacteriological testing before any home is connected to it.
  • Driveways stay usable except for short periods while the trench crosses them.
  • Trenches are patched temporarily and the streets receive a full-width overlay at the end of the project.

Hydrant Flushing, Zone 3 (north of Settling Pond Road)

Notice CN-26-15 | Posted October 15, 2026

District crews will flush water mains in Zone 3 from Monday, October 26, through Thursday, November 19, 2026, Monday to Thursday, 8:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. Flushing moves sediment out of the mains and lets crews check that each hydrant and valve works.

You may notice low pressure or discolored water for a short time. If the water is discolored, run the cold water tap nearest the meter until it clears, and avoid doing laundry until it does. The water remains safe to drink.

Lift Station No. 4 Rehabilitation, Greyspring Road

Notice CN-26-13 | Posted September 17, 2026

Replacement of both sewage pumps, the controls and the standby generator at Lift Station No. 4 began Monday, September 28, 2026, and is expected to be complete by Thursday, December 17, 2026. During the work, sewage is moved by temporary bypass pumps. Neighbors may hear the bypass pumps at night. One lane of Greyspring Road next to the station will be closed during working hours, with flaggers.

Sewer Manhole Rehabilitation, Pellham Cross Road - completed

Notice CN-26-11 | Posted August 20, 2026 | Updated September 24, 2026

Lining of 22 sewer manholes between Settling Pond Road and the Westmere town line was completed on Thursday, September 24, 2026. All lane closures have been removed.
