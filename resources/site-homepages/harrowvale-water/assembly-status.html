Assembly Status

No assembly found for that serial or account number.

Check the number and try again:

  • Enter the assembly serial number exactly as it is stamped on the assembly body or shown on your test notice, including any letters.
  • Account numbers are printed in the upper right corner of your water bill. Enter the number without the dashes.
  • Records for tests submitted in the last 15 business days may not yet appear.
  • Assemblies installed under a permit issued in the last 30 days are added to the record after the first test report is received.

If your assembly still does not appear, use the contact form and give the serial number, the service address and the date of the last test. Do not send a second test report for the same test.
