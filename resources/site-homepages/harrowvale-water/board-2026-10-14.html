5.2 Service Agreement No. 26-031, Academy City Research - Halloway Ridge Laboratory Campus, 2900 Fenwick Quarry Road.
The Halloway Ridge site is the overseas laboratory campus of a research organisation partnered with Academy City. The applicant requests one new 8-inch fire service and one new 4-inch domestic service for a laboratory building now under construction. Because the building will contain research laboratories with chemical process water and the fire sprinkler system will include an antifreeze loop serving the loading dock, staff has classified both connections as high hazard. The agreement requires a reduced pressure detector assembly (RPDA) on the fire service and a reduced pressure principle assembly (RP) for premises isolation on the domestic service, both installed above grade in heated enclosures at the property line, tested before service is turned on and annually after that. The applicant will pay capacity and connection charges of $84,316.00 under the current fee resolution.
Recommended action: approve the agreement and authorize the General Manager to sign it. (Staff: Yukimi Kazari, Cross-Connection Control Specialist; Priya Ostrander, District Engineer)
