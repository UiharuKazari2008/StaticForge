Board Agendas > Minutes, September 9, 2026

Minutes of the Regular Meeting of the Board of Directors

Wednesday, September 9, 2026
Board Room, District Office, 4100 Settling Pond Road, Harrow Vale
Approved by the Board on October 14, 2026.

1. Call to order and roll call

President Feldhaus called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m. Present: Directors Feldhaus, Albee, Ellingboe and Varga. Absent: Director Pask. Staff present: General Manager Huntsinger, District Engineer Ostrander, Finance Officer Daniel Corwin and Board Clerk Whitlow. Two members of the public attended.

2. Pledge of Allegiance

Led by Director Varga.

3. Public comment

Harold Pruitt, Tamsin Court, asked whether driveways in Dreamscape Estates would stay usable during the planned water main replacement. District Engineer Ostrander said access will be kept open except for short periods while the trench crosses each driveway, and residents will be notified at least 48 hours before any planned water shutoff.

4. Consent calendar

Items: (a) minutes of the regular meeting of August 12, 2026; (b) warrant register for August 2026, checks 48046 through 48210 and electronic transfers, total $587,931.12. Motion to approve by Director Albee, second by Director Ellingboe. Carried 4-0 (Pask absent).

5. Action items

5.1 Call for bids, Contract No. 2026-04, Dreamscape Estates Water Main Replacement, Phase 1. District Engineer Ostrander presented the plans and specifications and an engineer's estimate of $1,552,000.00. Bid opening was proposed for September 29, 2026, at 2:00 p.m. Motion to approve the plans and authorize the call for bids by Director Varga, second by Director Albee. Carried 4-0.

5.2 Resolution 2026-18, setting a public hearing on the 2027 sewer service charge report for December 9, 2026. Motion to adopt by Director Ellingboe, second by Director Varga. Carried 4-0.

6. Reports

General Manager Huntsinger reported that the Lift Station No. 4 pump replacement is scheduled to start September 28 and will need bypass pumping for about eleven weeks. He also reported that the District received an application from Academy City Research for new fire and domestic services at its Halloway Ridge laboratory site; a service agreement will be brought to the Board in October.

7. Director comments

Director Albee asked staff to post the Dreamscape Estates construction schedule on the website once the contract is awarded.

8. Adjournment

The meeting was adjourned at 7:08 p.m.

Respectfully submitted,
Janet Whitlow, Board Clerk
