Revisions policy

This policy explains when figures in the quarterly bulletins are revised and how revisions are shown.

Scheduled revisions

Each bulletin is compiled from inspection returns received by the twentieth working day after the end of the quarter. Returns received later are added to the figures for that quarter in the next bulletin. The previous quarter's figures may therefore be revised once, in the following bulletin. After that they are final unless an error is found.

Corrections

If an error is found in a published bulletin or dataset, a correction is published as soon as practicable, with a notice at the top of the affected bulletin explaining what was changed and why.

How revisions are shown

  • Revised figures are marked (r) in the bulletin that publishes them, with a short explanation under the heading "Revisions".
  • A bulletin whose figures have been revised later is not altered. A notice is added at the top giving the revised totals and the bulletin in which they appear.
  • Datasets are kept as first published. Revised figures are included in the datasets of the bulletin that publishes them.
