Quarterly Bulletin No. 116: Grain Moisture Meter Calibration Results, First Quarter 2026

Release date: Friday 8 May 2026, 09:30 | Reference period: 1 January to 31 March 2026 | Next release: Friday 14 August 2026 (Q2 2026) | Classification: Official statistics

This bulletin reports the results of calibration checks carried out by Service inspectors on in-service grain moisture meters at licensed receiving points during the quarter. Results are not seasonally adjusted.

Main points

  • 975 checks were made on 587 meters at 327 licensed receiving points.
  • 917 checks (94.1%) were within the maintenance tolerance of plus or minus 0.5 percentage points, compared with 93.2% in Q4 2025.
  • Yellow dent corn accounted for the largest number of checks (307). Oats had the lowest proportion within tolerance (85.5%).
  • The mean error across all checks was +0.02 pp, with a standard deviation of 0.27 pp.
  • Of the 58 checks outside tolerance, 41 were resolved on the day by adjustment and re-check and 17 resulted in the meter being withdrawn from service for that grain type.

Notes

  1. A check is one meter tested on one grain type against three reference samples. A meter tested on more than one grain type contributes one check for each.
  2. pp = percentage points of moisture content, wet basis. Mean error is the meter reading minus the reference value, so a positive figure means meters read high on average.
  3. A check is within tolerance when the mean of its three sample errors is within plus or minus 0.5 pp (Service Order MS-14, Schedule 2).
  4. Reference moisture values were determined by the air-oven method at the Academy City Research automated grain laboratory in School District 17, under contract to the Service.
  5. Meters outside tolerance were adjusted and re-checked, or withdrawn from service for that grain type, on the day of inspection. The tables count the first result of each check.
  6. Malting barley reference samples were prepared from the two-row spring cultivars Dreamscape and Marrowby (2025 harvest).
