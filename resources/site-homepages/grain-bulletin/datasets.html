Datasets

Each table in the online bulletins is available as a CSV file. Files are plain text, comma separated, with column names in the first row. The last row of each file is the total. Mean error and standard deviation are in percentage points of moisture, wet basis; see the methodology for definitions.

Datasets for Bulletins No. 115 and 116 are kept as first published. Revised Q1 2026 figures are in AMSB-117-T1 (last column) and AMSB-117-T3.
