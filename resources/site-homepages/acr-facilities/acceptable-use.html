Acceptable use of facilities systems

PolicyFAC-POL-012, version 2.1
OwnerFacilities and Site Services
Approved16 March 2026
Next reviewMarch 2028

1. Scope

This policy covers the Facilities Booking System, the room panels outside bookable rooms and the pool vehicle key cabinet.

2. Who may use the systems

ACR staff, and contractors with a current staff account. Rooms for visitors are booked by their host.

3. Making bookings

  • Book in your own name, or for a colleague with their agreement.
  • Book only the room size and time you need, and release a room you no longer need at least two hours before the start.
  • Recurring bookings run for up to 13 weeks and must then be renewed.
  • A room not checked in at its panel within 15 minutes of the start time is released automatically.

4. Pool vehicles

  • A current driving authorisation must be on record with Site Services before a vehicle can be booked.
  • Record the odometer reading at collection and return in the vehicle log.
  • Return electric vehicles on charge, and fuelled vehicles with at least a quarter of a tank.
  • Report any damage or warning light at the Site Services desk before the next booking.
  • Trips outside Academy City need a District 11 gate pass, requested through the Facilities Help Desk at least three working days before the trip.

5. AV equipment

Loan equipment is collected from and returned to the AV store. Loans are for up to five working days. Do not install software on loan laptops.

AV loans are suspended each year during the Daihaseisai (19 to 25 September), when the equipment is reserved for festival venues. Equipment already fitted in bookable rooms is not affected.

6. Accounts

Do not share your account. You are responsible for bookings made under it.

7. Records

Booking records are kept for 24 months. System activity is logged for audit and capacity planning, as described in the staff IT policy.

8. Misuse

Repeated no-shows or misuse may lead to booking rights being suspended by the Facilities Manager.
