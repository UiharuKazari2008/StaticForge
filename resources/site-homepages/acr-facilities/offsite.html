Working off-site

The Facilities Booking System is not published to the internet. To use it from home or while travelling:

  1. Connect to the staff remote access service with your ACR account and your sign-in token or authenticator app.
  2. Once connected, open the Facilities Booking System from the Staff Intranet home page, or reload this page.
  3. If the system still shows the internal network notice, close all browser windows and open the system again. Some browsers keep the earlier page for a while.

Not set up for remote access?

Remote access is requested by your line manager through IT Services. Instructions for installing and setting up the remote access client are in the Remote access setup guide on the IT Services intranet pages.

Changing a booking you cannot reach

If you cannot connect and need to change or cancel a booking for today, ring the Facilities Help Desk on internal extension 4410 from any office phone (from outside the network: +1 (997) 434-2600, ext. 4410), or send a note through the help desk form. Pool vehicle keys are issued at the Site Services desk in Main Building reception whether or not the booking can be viewed remotely.
