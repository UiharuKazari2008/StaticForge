                                    Exit Desktop on Workspace Maximise
                                    
When off, maximize keeps desktop mode and expands the workspace within the work area.

                                    Open workspace on startup
                                    
When off, loading continues in the tray and the workspace window stays closed until you open it from the start menu or taskbar.

                                    Enable Notification Bridge

                                    Bypass Notification Bridge in Desktop Mode
