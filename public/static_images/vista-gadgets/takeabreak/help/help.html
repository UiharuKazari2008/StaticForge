Introduction

Good working practice states that you should take a break at regular intervals. This break doesn't have to be very long, between about a minute and five minutes is typical depending upon your occupation and how often you take a break.

The sorts of things you should do during these short breaks are:

  • Stretch
  • Move a round to stretch your limbs
  • May be get something to drink or eat (bearing in mind dietary concerns and constraints)

Quite often we become totally engrossed in what we are doing that we forget to take that break so Take-A-Break will remind you to take a break.

Read on to see how to use and customise Take-A-Break.

Using Take-A-Break

Take-A-Break is extremely easy to use. Just add it to your Windows Vista Sidebar, if required alter the settings and that's it! You can even run multiple Take-A-Break gadgets simultaneously each with their own settings.

When it is time to take a break the gadget will become opaque, play a sound and a message will be displayed. You can switch off the transparency and sound if you wish.

While loaded into your Sidebar, Take-A-Break will start counting from the time you turn on or log into your computer until you switch it off and will remind you to take those all important breaks.

Settings

Enable sound
When ticked, Take-A-Break will play the sound specified by the sound file setting. If the sound file cannot be played, Take-A-Break will play the default Beep. When not ticked no sound will be played.

This setting is dependant upon a fully functional sound system on your computer.

Fade In
When ticked the Take-A-Break gadget is displayed semi-transparent until it is time for a break. During the break the transparency is removed and the gadget is opaque.

When not ticked the gadget is always displayed opaque (i.e. not transparent).

Help

OK
Press the OK button to save the settings.

Note: Pressing OK will also reset the timers for your breaks. This may cause the message and sound to be played when the settings page closes.

Cancel
Press the Cancel button to abandon any changes.

Note: Pressing Cancel will not reset the timers.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q.    Can I run more than one Take-A-Break gadget at a time?
A.    Yes. You can run as many Take-A-Break gadgets as your computer can handle each with their own settings.

Q.    I can't hear a sound when it is time to take a break.
A.    Make sure that:

  • The Enable sounds checkbox is ticked
  • Your computer can play sounds
  • Your volume is turned up and isn't muted
  • You have entered the sound file correctly - use the Sound File Dialogue box to locate the sound file.

Q.    Will Take-A-Break run on other operating systems like Windows XP?
A.    Take-A-Break, at present, only runs on Windows Vista.

Q.    Can I change the any of the other settings like the messages and timings etc?
A.    We hope to have an enhanced Take-A-Break gadget available shortly. Please check the web site www.jqlgadget.com for when this will be available.

Q.    Is Take-A-Break available in other languages?
A.    Yes! Take-A-Break is available in English, French and German though, at present the help file is only available in English. More translations are planed please check the web site www.jqlgadget.com for when these become available.

Q.    Do you have any other gadgets?
A.    We are currently working on some new gadgets. Please see the web site www.jqlgadget.com for more details.
