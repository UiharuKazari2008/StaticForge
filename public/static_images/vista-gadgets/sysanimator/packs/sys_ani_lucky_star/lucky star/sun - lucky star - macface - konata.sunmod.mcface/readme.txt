This is the same version available on the author's website, but with some cleanup of stray pixels and improvements to the bustline crop on the base images.  These become really apparent on desktops with dark backgounds.  I've also modified faceDef.plist files to show the full character names in Japanese text as well as included an English faceDef.plist for any English fans out there.

To get the MacFace in English you have to rename/replace the original faceDef.plist with the English one before loading it into your MacFace program.

Many props to the original author and one question.  Why no Miyuki?
